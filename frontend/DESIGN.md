# Frontend Design Direction

- Identity: a calm, professional workspace for scanning IT job listings.
- Palette: light, paper-like surfaces with dark ink and a restrained green-teal accent.
- Typography: system sans-serif for efficient reading and compact information.
- Composition: prioritize job title, company, location, and qualifications; avoid unrelated dashboard metrics.
- Dials: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Motion is limited to brief interaction feedback.
- Assets: use the product name as text and a user-requested briefcase/check app mark in the existing teal accent; no avatars or illustrative artwork.
